# Lovely Runner

## My Favorite Series

**Lovely Runner** is my favorite Korean drama series. It tells the story of Im Sol, who gets a chance to travel back in time and tries to save Ryu Sun-jae, her favorite idol.

### Why I Like It
- The story is unique and interesting.
- The characters are easy to relate to.
- The mix of romance and time travel keeps the series exciting.
- The soundtrack is really good.

### Main Characters
1. Im Sol
2. Ryu Sun-jae
3. Kim Tae-sung

> One thing I learned from this series is that every choice can have a big impact on a person's future.

**Rating:** 5/5 ⭐

*I would recommend this series to anyone who enjoys romance and fantasy dramas.*
